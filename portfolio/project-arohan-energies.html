Arohan Energies

Solar Energy Solutions & Digital Lead Generation Platform

Turning Solar Energy into a Smarter Digital Experience

Arohan Energies is a modern solar-energy website designed to help residential, commercial and industrial customers understand solar solutions, estimate potential savings and connect with the solar team.

The platform combines a premium corporate experience with interactive tools such as a solar savings calculator, solar panel cleaning service estimator and WhatsApp-based lead flow.

About the Project

Arohan Energies is a digital platform created for a solar-energy business focused on residential, commercial and industrial solar solutions. The website was designed to communicate the value of solar energy clearly while giving potential customers practical tools to understand their investment.

Instead of relying only on traditional marketing sections, the website introduces interactive experiences that help customers move from discovering solar to calculating potential savings and contacting the business.

Solar Savings Calculator Helps visitors estimate system size, generation and potential savings.
WhatsApp Lead Flow Converts website enquiries into pre-filled WhatsApp conversations.
Solar Cleaning Service Customers can understand cleaning pricing based on their system size.
Responsive Experience Designed for desktop, tablet and mobile visitors.

Customer Experience

The main goal was to make the website useful rather than simply informational. Visitors can immediately understand what Arohan Energies does, explore relevant solar solutions and use interactive tools before contacting the company.

The cleaning-service experience was also designed around pricing transparency. Instead of simply displaying “₹250/kW”, the interface explains what a visit means, calculates the expected charge and shows examples such as a 3 kW system.

Rooftop Solar Panel Cleaning

One-time cleaning: ₹250/kW per visit

Annual-plan rate: ₹200/kW per visit

Example: 3 kW × ₹250 = ₹750 per visit

The calculator allows customers to enter their system capacity and understand the estimated charge before making an enquiry.

Why It Matters

Solar customers often need answers to practical questions before they are ready to contact a provider: How large should the system be? How much could it generate? What could the savings look like? What services are available after installation?

Arohan's website addresses these questions directly through interactive tools and transparent service information.

“Make solar easier to understand, evaluate and act on.”
